Booking Process FAQ

Everything you need to know about our hassle-free booking, airport pickup protocol, payment methods, and one-way outstation cab rates.

1. How do I book a Chennai airport taxi with The All India Travels?
+
Booking is straightforward and takes less than 60 seconds. You can either call us directly at +91 93635 39853, send a WhatsApp message, or submit the booking form above. Simply provide your travel date, pickup location or terminal, destination, and flight number. Our 24/7 dispatch manager will instantly send you an all-inclusive transparent fare quote, and confirm your booking with driver details dispatched well before your scheduled time.
2. Where will the cab driver meet me at Chennai International Airport?
+
For domestic arrivals (Terminal 1) and international arrivals (Terminals 2 & 4), our chauffeur will wait at the official passenger arrival pickup lane right outside the terminal gate. Prior to landing, you receive the chauffeur's name, mobile number, and vehicle registration plate. Our dispatch desk continuously monitors your live flight status on radar, so even if your flight lands early or is delayed by several hours, your cab will be parked and waiting with zero waiting fees.
3. Can I book a one-way outstation taxi without paying round-trip fares?
+
Yes, absolutely! We are Tamil Nadu's leading dedicated one-way taxi specialist. Whether you are traveling from Chennai Airport to Pondicherry, Tirupati, Vellore, Madurai, Trichy, Kumbakonam, Bangalore, or Salem, you only pay for the exact one-way travel distance. There is no return toll fee, no empty return mileage charge, and no hidden driver night penalties.
4. What payment methods do you accept, and are there hidden charges?
+
We pride ourselves on 100% upfront pricing transparency. What we quote is exactly what you pay. We accept all major payment modes including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, Credit/Debit cards, and cash payment directly to the chauffeur upon arrival. There are zero surge prices, zero cancellation fees when canceled with advance notice, and no unexpected toll surprises.
