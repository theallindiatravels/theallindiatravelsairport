One-Way Drop vs Round-Trip Cab

Not sure whether to choose a One-Way Drop or Round-Trip Outstation Taxi? Here is how both options compare so you can save maximum money and travel comfortably.

Option A

One-Way Drop Taxi

Ideal when you are visiting a city with an open-ended return date, catching a flight from another airport, or relocating.

  • ✓ Pay only for 1-way distance: Zero return fuel or vehicle empty run charges.
  • ✓ No minimum km penalty: Standard slab rate based strictly on Chennai to destination route.
  • ✓ No Driver Night Halt charges: Once dropped, your bill ends immediately.
  • ✓ Door-to-door convenience: Picked from your Chennai home/airport and dropped at exact address.
  • ℹ Best for: College joinings, one-way business meetings, returning to hometown.
Book One-Way Drop
Option B

Round-Trip Outstation Cab

Best when you are heading out for weekend family vacations, multiple temple darshans, or sightseeing hill stations.

  • ✓ Dedicated vehicle at your disposal: Cab and driver stay with you throughout the holiday.
  • ✓ Local sightseeing included: Stop at waterfalls, temples, viewpoints, and restaurants at leisure.
  • ✓ Cost-effective for multi-day tours: Billed at standard 250 - 300 km/day base.
  • ✓ Flexibility to change itinerary: Add en-route halts without re-booking tickets.
  • ℹ Best for: Weekend getaways, Navagraha temple circuits, Ooty & Kodaikanal holidays.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding Chennai airport drop fares, outstation bookings, toll payments, and cancellation policies.

How do I book a Chennai Airport taxi in advance? +
You can book in under 60 seconds! Simply send your flight arrival date, time, and drop destination to our WhatsApp at +91 93635 39853 or call us. Our driver will be assigned with car number and tracking link prior to your touchdown.
Are there midnight or peak-hour surge charges? +
No. Unlike app-based aggregators with 2x or 3x dynamic pricing in heavy rain or midnight hours, The All India Travels provides locked, flat quotes confirmed prior to vehicle departure.
What if my flight arrives late at Chennai Airport? +
We monitor live flight radars. If your inbound flight is delayed by an hour or more, your driver adjusts arrival time automatically at no additional waiting fee.
Are tolls, GST, and interstate taxes included in the fare? +
Our tariff chart quotes base vehicle and chauffeur charges. Fastag tolls and interstate border road permits (for Tirupati in AP, Munnar in Kerala, or Bangalore in Karnataka) are billed on actuals, or can be bundled into a single all-inclusive fixed package upon request.
Can I make en-route stops for breakfast or sightseeing? +
Absolutely! For one-way outstation cabs, reasonable tea and meal breaks on highway plazas are warmly accommodated. For round-trip bookings, full local sightseeing is included within your daily mileage.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, UPI, Bank Transfer (IMPS/NEFT), and direct Cash to driver upon successful completion of your journey.
