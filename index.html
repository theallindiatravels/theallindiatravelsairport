❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Chennai Airport Taxi — Common Questions

How do I book a taxi from Chennai Airport?
You can book your Chennai airport taxi instantly via WhatsApp at +91 93635 39853 or call us directly. Share your flight details, pickup time, and destination — we'll confirm your booking with fare details within minutes. No app download required.
What are the taxi rates from Chennai Airport?
Our Chennai airport taxi rates start from ₹499 for city drops (T. Nagar, Anna Nagar, Adyar). Outstation fares start at ₹9/km for sedan and ₹14/km for Innova. One-way taxi to Pondicherry starts at ₹2,199. All fares are transparent with no hidden charges or surge pricing.
Do you offer one-way taxi from Chennai?
Yes! We offer one-way taxi service from Chennai to all major destinations including Pondicherry, Tirupati, Madurai, Coimbatore, Bangalore, Ooty, and more. You only pay for one side — no compulsory return fare. This saves you up to 50% compared to round-trip charges.
Is the taxi service available 24/7?
Absolutely! Our Chennai airport taxi service operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. Whether your flight lands at 3 AM or you need a 5 AM airport drop, we are always available. Early morning and late-night rides are our specialty.
What car types are available for booking?
We offer Sedan (Swift Dzire, Toyota Etios), SUV (Toyota Innova, Innova Crysta), and Tempo Traveller (12-16 seater) for group bookings. All vehicles are AC-equipped, GPS-tracked, and regularly sanitized for your comfort and safety.
What if my flight is delayed?
We track your flight in real-time and adjust the driver's arrival time accordingly. There are no extra waiting charges for flight delays up to 45 minutes. Your driver will be ready at the arrival terminal when you land. Just WhatsApp us your PNR or flight number.
