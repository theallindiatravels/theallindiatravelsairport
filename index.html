Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Chennai Airport taxi bookings, one-way tariffs, fleet options, and flight delay policies.

How do I book an airport taxi from Chennai Airport (MAA)?
+
Booking is instant and simple! You can click our WhatsApp button or call +91 93635 39853 with your flight number and arrival time. Our dispatch team will immediately send your driver name, car number, and live tracking link before you exit the terminal gates.
What happens if my flight to Chennai is delayed?
+
We monitor all incoming domestic and international flight statuses in real time. If your flight is delayed or rescheduled, your chauffeur adjusts arrival accordingly. There are zero extra waiting charges or cancellation penalties for flight delays.
How does One-Way Outstation Taxi work? Do I pay return fare?
+
No! With our specialized One-Way Taxi service, you only pay for the exact one-side distance traveled from Chennai Airport to your destination (such as Pondicherry, Vellore, Tirupati, or Bangalore). You never pay for empty return kilometers.
Are your taxi fares fixed or metered? Are tolls included?
+
We provide 100% upfront, transparent fares with no hidden surcharges. Our package rates clearly specify whether highway toll gates, parking, and driver allowances are included so you never face unpleasant surprises at journey end.
What vehicle models are available in your Chennai cab fleet?
+
We maintain an extensive, pristine fleet: Comfort Sedans (Maruti Dzire, Hyundai Aura, Honda Amaze), Premium SUVs (Toyota Innova Crysta, Maruti Ertiga), and Luxury Urbania / Tempo Travelers (12 to 20 seaters) for large tourist groups.
Do I need to book in advance or are cabs ready on the spot?
+
While we always recommend booking 2 to 4 hours in advance for guaranteed vehicle preference, we maintain ready standby cabs at Chennai International Airport (Meenambakkam) available 24/7 for instant on-demand dispatches.
