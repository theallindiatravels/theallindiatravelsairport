Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Chennai taxi booking, airport pickup protocols, and outstation fares.

How does airport pickup work at Chennai International Airport (MAA)?
+
When you book an airport pickup with us, please share your flight number. Our dispatch team continuously monitors your flight landing time. Once you land, our chauffeur will be stationed at the designated arrivals terminal (Terminal 1 for Domestic, Terminal 2 for International, or Terminal 4) holding a nameboard. We provide 45 minutes of complimentary waiting time after flight touchdown for luggage collection.
How does One-Way Taxi save money compared to normal taxis?
+
Traditional cab operators bill you for two-way kilometers (round-trip fare) even if you are only dropping off at Pondicherry, Tirupati, or Bangalore. With The All India Travels Airport One-Way Drop service, you are billed strictly for the one-way distance travelled. This saves you anywhere between 30% to 45% on outstation travel costs.
Are toll charges, driver batta, and state permits included?
+
Our quotations are 100% transparent. We provide both all-inclusive packages (where tolls, parking, and driver batta are bundled) and per-km pricing plans where tolls and parking are paid at actual toll gates. For outstation multi-day trips, driver batta (night allowance) is explicitly quoted upfront with zero unexpected demands.
Can we request multiple sightseeing stops during a temple or holiday tour?
+
Yes, absolutely! On our temple tour and holiday outstation packages, the vehicle and driver remain dedicated exclusively to your group. You can adjust your daily itinerary, pause for scenic photography, visit nearby temples, and stop at clean highway food courts whenever you desire.
What happens if my flight is delayed late into the night?
+
Do not worry at all! Our dispatch system operates 24 hours a day, 365 days a year right next to Chennai International Airport. Since our drivers track live flight statuses, your cab will automatically be rescheduled to match your actual touchdown time with zero penalty or cancellation fees.
What payment modes do you accept?
+
We offer complete payment flexibility. You can pay via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM UPI, Net Banking, or direct cash to the chauffeur upon arrival. For corporate clients, we provide GST tax invoices with 15 or 30-day billing cycles.
